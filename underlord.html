Underlord

The heroes of the surface world are coming for your Dungeon Heart. Dig into the dark, build rooms, attract creatures and survive six invasions.

  • Click a block to mark it for digging. Drag to mark a whole area at once. Your imps dig, haul gold and claim new ground.
  • The map is hidden in clouds, but gold seams and portals shine through. Mark blocks anywhere, even in the clouds, and dig to find treasure and wandering creatures.
  • Two portals are always visible. Claim one to attract creatures. Each creature needs a free bed in your Lair; stronger creatures also need bigger rooms. Each map has a limited pool of creatures.
  • Pick creatures up with the hand and drop them where the fighting is. Right-click slaps: slapped imps work faster.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom. Drag with the right or middle mouse button, or use the arrow keys or WASD, to look around.
